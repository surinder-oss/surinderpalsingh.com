The Corporate Wealth Playbook

Your mortgage is one piece of a bigger plan. This is how I explain the path from company profit to family wealth, in plain English, in the order most families build it.

11%Tax an Alberta small business pays on its first $500K of active profit. That head start is where the plan begins.

  1. 1

    Own it personally

    Your home stays in your own name. The gain when you sell it is tax-free, and it's the easiest property to finance.

  2. 2

    Earn it in your company

    Profit you leave in the company has only been taxed at the small-business rate, so far more of it can be invested.

  3. 3

    Park it in a HoldCo

    Surplus profit can usually move up to a holding company tax-free, away from the operating company's risks.

  4. 4

    Grow it

    Real estate and long-term investments inside the HoldCo. Mortgages for company-owned property work differently, and that's where I come in.

  5. 5

    Pass it on

    A family trust and an estate freeze can send future growth to the next generation instead of the tax bill.

  6. 6

    Insure and unlock it

    Corporate-owned life insurance pays the company, and much of it can reach the family tax-free through the capital dividend account.

This is education, not tax, legal or insurance advice. Every structure is designed and signed off by your CPA and tax lawyer. I arrange the financing and work alongside them.
